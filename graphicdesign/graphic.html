Fungus
Editorial & Bookbinding
05 2024

A book transforming Wikapedia’s digital entry on Fungus into print. The images function like the fungus itself—gradually growing in size and
spreading across the pages. As they expand, they begin to overtake the space, forcing the text to shrink in response. This visual tension mimics the behavior of fungus, consuming its environment and reshaping the balance between image and type.

These posters interpret the lyrics of Lana Del Rey’s Video Games through typography. Using only the typeface Courier, each poster explores repetition and investigates how typography can be pushed to create new forms, rhythms, and a sense of movement. The designs translate the emotional qualities of the lyrics into a visual language through the manipulation, layering, and repetition of type.
