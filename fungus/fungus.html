Fungus

book design

A book transforming Wikapedia’s digital entry on Fungus into print. The images function like the fungus itself—gradually growing in size and spreading across the pages. As they expand, they begin to overtake the space, forcing the text to shrink in response. This visual tension mimics the behavior of fungus, consuming its environment and reshaping the balance between image and type.
